System Overview

Cutting-edge edge intelligence built for zero-latency human-wildlife conflict mitigation.

Real-Time IoT Monitoring

Multi-node ESP32 mesh nodes with acoustic decibel sensors, PIR motion detectors, and GPS coordinates streaming live to cloud and dashboard.

Edge-AI Acoustic & Vision

Dual-stage confirmation layer fusing infrasonic elephant rumble acoustics and edge camera verification to eliminate false alarms.

Early Warning & Rapid Alerts

Instantaneous multi-channel dispatch via Web Push, SMS, and WhatsApp alerts notifying villagers and response teams within seconds.

Wildlife & Community Safety

Humane non-lethal bio-acoustic deterrents (bee hive sounds, strobe beacons) gently steering elephant herds back toward sanctuary zones.

How It Works

End-to-end autonomous flow from corridor sensor to emergency community response.

01

IoT Sensing

Field ESP32 nodes capture PIR movement and ambient acoustic levels in high-risk border corridors.

02

AI Verification

Acoustic and camera models process incoming signals to confirm genuine elephant presence.

03

Cloud Dispatch

Supabase syncs telemetry and triggers synchronized role-specific notifications instantly.

04

Targeted Action

Villagers stay indoors, police manage road perimeters, and forest rangers deploy humane deterrents.
