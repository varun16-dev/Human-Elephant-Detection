Community Safety Protocols

Official wildlife encounter guidelines approved by Karnataka Forest Department.

What YOU MUST DO

  • Remain Indoors: When an alert is active, stay inside sturdy concrete or brick homes.
  • Keep Bright Lights On: Illuminate exterior compound solar lamps to deter elephants from crossing fences.
  • Protect Livestock: Bring cows and domestic animals into covered, locked pens before dusk.
  • Alert Neighbors: Inform nearby farm houses via phone or village public address system.

What YOU MUST NEVER DO

  • Do NOT Confront Elephants: Never approach herds or take flash photographs; elephants perceive flash as a threat.
  • Do NOT Throw Firecrackers or Stones: Provoking wild elephants causes panic charges and severe property damage.
  • Do NOT Block Corridor Paths: Avoid standing or parking tractors across natural forest fringe animal routes.
  • Do NOT Venture Out at Night: Never walk alone into crop fields between 7:00 PM and 6:00 AM during alert status.
